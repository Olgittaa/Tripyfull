# Tripyfull — Product Overview

## 1. What is the product?

**In one sentence:** Tripyfull is a tool for travelers to plan trips in fine detail —
budgeting, day-by-day itineraries and booking management, the way a tour operator would.

**Problem:** Planning a trip is overwhelming when you want to account for everything but
don't know where to start.

**Why now / why us:** Most apps solve just one piece of the problem. Tripyfull takes a
comprehensive approach and brings the whole trip together in one place.

---

## 2. Personas

### Persona: Darina, 30, blogger

- **Who they are:** travels 3–4 times a year, likes to map out the whole itinerary in detail beforehand
- **Context:** plans in the evenings on a laptop, checks details from her phone while traveling
- **Goals:** build the trip day by day without losing bookings or blowing the budget
- **Pains and frustrations:** bookings scattered across email, budget in a separate spreadsheet — everything drifts
  apart
- **Technical level:** beginner; has never used a travel-planning tool before
- **What "success" means to them:** the whole trip in one place, with the plan and remaining budget visible at a glance
- **Key quote:** "I want to see the whole itinerary and how much I can still spend — on one screen"

---

### Persona: Helga, 25, accountant

- **Who they are:** travels twice a year, prefers 2+ week trips and likes to explore an entire region
- **Context:** plans on a desktop, then checks sights and tracks the budget from her phone while traveling
- **Goals:** build a day-by-day itinerary and track bookings with future payments — she plans well in advance and
  doesn't always pay for everything upfront
- **Pains and frustrations:** there's no way to map out the route she needs to follow, and planning in Notion involves a
  lot of repetitive setup
- **Technical level:** advanced
- **What "success" means to them:** everything under control — plan it at home, then just follow it while traveling
- **Key quote:** "Everything under control, in one place"

## 3. Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

#### JTBD: Plan the itinerary day by day

- **Job story:** When I'm getting ready for a trip, I want to lay out activities across the
  days, so I can gauge whether the plan is realistic and not forget anything.
- **Who it concerns:** Darina
- **Current workaround:** notes plus a map in the browser, nothing connected
- **Definition of done:** trip → days → activities shown as a single timeline
- **Priority:** high

#### JTBD: Keep bookings and payments under control

- **Job story:** When I book lodging and tickets, I want to see all my bookings with their
  payment schedule, so I don't miss a payment or lose a confirmation.
- **Who it concerns:** Darina
- **Current workaround:** emails in the inbox, calendar reminders
- **Definition of done:** bookings + payments + attachments in one place under the right trip
- **Priority:** high

#### JTBD: Export the whole trip

- **Job story:** When I've planned my trip, I want to export the whole itinerary — a PDF to
  print, the daily routes to a maps app and hotels to my calendar — so everything is
  available even offline.
- **Who it concerns:** Helga
- **Current workaround:** manual copy-pasting, Notion
- **Definition of done:** bookings and itinerary can be exported
- **Priority:** high

#### JTBD: Track the trip while traveling

- **Job story:** When my trip begins, I want to track it in real time — logging expenses,
  photos and notes — so I can see how things are actually going.
- **Who it concerns:** Helga
- **Current workaround:** nothing
- **Definition of done:** actual expenses compared against the plan, with photos and notes captured
- **Priority:** medium

---

## 4. Boundaries (what the product does NOT do)

- Booking directly through us
- Partnerships / affiliate deals
- Searching for cheaper flights or hotels
- Ranking or rating sightseeing spots

---

## 5. Success metrics

- **Activation:** a user creates their first trip with ≥ 2 days
- **Retention:** the user comes back to plan a second trip
- **Key action:** new trips created and new users signing up
